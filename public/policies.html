Welcome to Viaara Travels.

Please read the following policies carefully before making a booking.

1. Terms & Conditions

Draft for review. Not approved for publication.

Last updated: November 20, 2026

These Terms & Conditions apply to participants booking and joining trips organized by Viaara Travels. By completing a booking and participating in a trip, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and accepted these conditions.

1.1 Booking & Registration

  • A booking is considered confirmed only after successful registration and payment of the applicable booking amount.
  • Participants must provide accurate registration details and the required identification documents.
  • The booking amount forms part of the total trip package cost wherever specified in the respective itinerary.
  • The registration amount is non-refundable where identified as such in the respective trip package.
  • The remaining trip balance must be paid before departure. Individual trip payment instructions may specify payment of the remaining amount at departure time.
  • Failure to complete the required payment may result in cancellation of the booking without a refund.

1.2 Identification & Travel Documents

  • Every participant must carry a valid government-issued photo identification document throughout the journey.
  • International travellers must carry a valid passport and visa.
  • Participants must carry the same identification document used during registration.
  • Travel tickets are non-transferable.
  • Participants below 18 years of age must submit a signed No Objection Certificate (NOC) from a parent or legal guardian.

1.3 Accommodation

  • Accommodation is provided on a sharing basis, according to the package and availability.
  • Room-sharing arrangements depend on the selected package, such as double, triple, or quad sharing.
  • Accommodation is subject to availability.
  • Air-conditioned rooms may not be available at hill destinations.

1.4 Transportation & Travel Arrangements

  • Transportation and transfers are provided according to the inclusions mentioned in the respective trip itinerary.
  • Participants must follow the designated pickup points, departure schedules, and instructions communicated for their trip.
  • Certain destinations have road restrictions or vehicle-access limitations.
  • In such cases, the main vehicle may be parked at a designated parking area, and participants may need to continue on foot or use local transportation.
  • Local transportation required outside the planned arrangements may be at the participant's own expense, as specified in the itinerary.

1.5 Itinerary & Schedule Changes

  • Trip itineraries and schedules may change due to weather conditions, road conditions, safety concerns, operational requirements, or other unforeseen circumstances.
  • Certain sightseeing locations, mountain routes, treks, and activities may be subject to weather conditions, road accessibility, snowfall, permits, and government regulations.
  • Viaara Travels may modify the itinerary when necessary for operational or safety reasons.
  • Participants acknowledge that planned activities and schedules may be affected by circumstances beyond the control of Viaara Travels.

1.6 Health, Fitness & Medical Disclosure

  • Participants must ensure that they are medically fit to undertake the selected trip.
  • Any pre-existing medical condition, allergy, or special requirement must be disclosed to Viaara Travels before the trip.
  • Participants are responsible for any undisclosed medical condition or emergency arising during the journey.
  • Travel insurance is not included in the trip package. Participants are advised to arrange their own travel insurance.

1.7 Participant Responsibilities & Conduct

Every participant is expected to maintain responsible and respectful behaviour throughout the journey.

Participants must:

  • Follow instructions issued by the Trip Captain and authorized representatives of Viaara Travels.
  • Follow applicable instructions and safety requirements issued by local authorities.
  • Respect fellow travellers, local communities, accommodation providers, and other service personnel.
  • Take responsibility for their personal belongings, valuables, travel documents, and luggage.
  • Avoid behaviour that may compromise the safety, comfort, or experience of other participants.

Misconduct, harassment, illegal activities, alcohol or drug abuse, or damage to property may result in immediate removal from the trip without any refund.

Viaara Travels reserves the right to refuse or discontinue participation on medical, safety, legal, or behavioural grounds.

1.8 Activities & Third-Party Services

  • Optional activities operated by third-party vendors are undertaken at the participant's own discretion and risk.
  • Any activity or service not specifically listed under the package inclusions is not included in the package price.
  • Participants are responsible for understanding the arrangements and applicable charges for optional activities.

1.9 Inclusions & Exclusions

Each trip has its own specific list of inclusions and exclusions.

Participants should review the respective itinerary before booking.

Services not specifically mentioned under the inclusions section should be considered excluded from the package.

Additional expenses may include, where applicable:

  • Personal expenses.
  • Meals not mentioned in the itinerary.
  • Entry tickets and activity charges.
  • Local transportation outside planned arrangements.
  • Private cabs.
  • Personal trekking gear and equipment.
  • Additional club or water-sports charges.
  • Expenses arising from weather conditions, roadblocks, or natural calamities.

The exact inclusions and exclusions depend on the selected trip.

1.10 Delays, Disruptions & Unforeseen Circumstances

Viaara Travels is not responsible for delays, cancellations, route changes, accidents, injuries, loss of belongings, or additional expenses arising from circumstances beyond its control, including:

  • Adverse weather conditions.
  • Traffic delays.
  • Strikes.
  • Natural disasters.
  • Government restrictions.
  • Other unforeseen circumstances.

1.11 Missed Services & Activities

No refund will be provided for services, activities, meals, or sightseeing missed due to personal reasons or late arrival.

1.12 Assumption of Risk & Participant Declaration

Adventure travel and group journeys involve inherent risks, including accidents, illness, injuries, delays, weather conditions, altitude-related challenges, and unforeseen circumstances.

By participating in a trip, participants acknowledge these risks and voluntarily accept responsibility for their participation.

Participants confirm that they are medically fit for the selected trip and agree to follow all instructions issued by the Trip Captain and Viaara Travels representatives.

Participants also acknowledge that they are responsible for their personal belongings, valuables, documents, and luggage throughout the journey.

The participant declaration includes an assumption of risk and an indemnity in favour of Viaara Travels, its employees, trip leaders, and representatives, subject to applicable law.
